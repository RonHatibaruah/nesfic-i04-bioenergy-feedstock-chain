Field 1: Problem understanding & user workflow
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(Word Count: ~445 words / Max: 500 words)

[ Problem Context & Scale ]
Agriculture is the foundational economic bedrock of Assam, employing over 70% of the rural workforce across 28+ lakh operational holdings spanning 35 districts and 6 agro-climatic zones (Upper, Central, Lower Brahmaputra Valleys, North Bank Plain, Hills, and Barak Valley). The state's staple crops—Sali (winter) paddy, Boro (summer) paddy, Ahu (autumn) paddy, rapeseed/mustard, jute, and seasonal vegetables—are subject to severe micro-climatic volatility, recurrent monsoon floods, heavy prolonged morning dews, and high ambient relative humidity (>85%). 

These specific agro-ecological conditions create breeding corridors for devastating biotic pathogens and pests, most notably:
1. Rice Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae), causing up to 35–40% leaf and neck blast crop destruction.
2. Yellow Stem Borer (Scirpophaga incertulas), yielding extensive vegetative "dead-hearts" and reproductive "white-heads".
3. Sheath Blight (Rhizoctonia solani) and Bacterial Leaf Blight (Xanthomonas oryzae), spreading rapidly across standing waterlogged paddy fields.
4. Mustard Aphids (Lipaphis erysimi), decimating winter oilseed flowers during overcast, rainless intervals.

[ Current Workflow & Its Structural Gaps ]
Currently, agricultural disease management operates in a purely reactive firefighting mode. Infestations are recognized only after severe visual foliar damage or dead-hearts appear across whole village clusters. By this stage, economic thresholds (ETL) have already been breached.

The current administrative workflow exhibits three structural bottlenecks:
  • Severe Extension Ratio Deficit: A single Agricultural Development Officer (ADO) or Village Level Extension Worker (VLEW) oversees 15–20 villages comprising thousands of fragmented smallholder plots, rendering timely manual physical scouting physically impossible.
  • Generic District-Level Advisories: Current advisories broadcast broad district-wide bulletins (e.g. "spray pesticide if pests appear"), lacking block-level, plot-specific, or micro-climatic precision.
  • Uncoordinated Data Silos: High-resolution Earth observation data at the Assam State Space Application Centre (ASSAC), IMD automated weather station telemetry, and AAU agricultural research packages remain uncoupled from real-time farmer delivery channels.

[ Users & Beneficiaries ]
  • Primary Departmental Users: Directorate of Agriculture, District Agricultural Officers (DAOs), Sub-Divisional Agricultural Officers (SDAOs), ADOs, and Krishi Sakhis.
  • Scientific & Remote Sensing Teams: Spatial data scientists at ASSAC, remote sensing analysts, and plant pathologists at Assam Agricultural University (AAU) Jorhat and regional Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs).
  • Ultimate Beneficiaries: Smallholder and marginal farming families of Assam, who receive timely, preventive 72-hour early warnings and actionable bilingual advisories in Assamese (অসমীয়া) and English.

[ Intended Outcome ]
SasyaSwasthya inverts this reactive cycle into a proactive, space-to-ground precision intelligence pipeline. By fusing Sentinel-2 10m multispectral satellite imagery, drone orthomosaics, and IMD micro-climatic grids with epidemiological AI models, it enables 72-hour pre-symptomatic pathogen warnings, slashes pesticide input costs by 30%, and protects rural farm household livelihoods.

Field 2: Proposed solution & key features
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(Word Count: ~460 words / Max: 500 words)

[ Proposed Solution Overview ]
SasyaSwasthya (শস্য স্বাস্থ্য) is an intelligent, space-to-ground crop health monitoring, pest forecasting, and location-specific advisory system tailored specifically for Assam's agro-climatic terrain. Operating as an end-to-end digital public infrastructure alongside the Department of Agriculture and ASSAC, it continuously monitors crop health at rest and dynamically models pathogen outbreaks before widespread physical crop loss occurs.

[ Main Modules & Core Technological Capabilities ]

1. Space-to-Ground Data Fusion Pipeline:
  • Multi-Spectral Satellite Telemetry: Ingests 5-day revisit ESA Sentinel-2A/2B and Landsat 8/9 imagery, calculating Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI), and Soil-Adjusted Vegetation Index (SAVI) at 10m spatial resolution with automated Scene Classification Layer (SCL) cloud-masking.
  • High-Resolution Drone Orthomosaics: Integrates ASSAC UAV drone multispectral flights at 5cm/pixel resolution over high-risk blocks for micro-canopy chlorosis detection.
  • Meteorological Grid Coupling: Ingests real-time feeds from 35+ IMD Automated Weather Stations (AWS) and block rain gauges (morning/evening relative humidity, minimum temperatures, precipitation accumulation, and leaf wetness hours).

2. Epidemiological Pest & Disease Early-Warning Engine:
  • Fungal Pathogen Models: Employs thermodynamic degree-day and moisture algorithms for Rice Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae), triggering automated alerts when morning relative humidity exceeds 88% and night temperatures hover between 20°C–26°C for $\ge 10$ continuous hours.
  • Insect Emergence Forecasting: Models Yellow Stem Borer larval eclosion and adult moth emergence curves correlated with rainfall onset during active vegetative tillering.
  • Composite Crop Health Index (CHI 0–100): Ranks every agricultural plot into 4 actionable risk tiers: Grade A (Optimal Vigor / 80–100), Grade B (Mild Stress / 65–79), Grade C (High-Risk Watchlist / 45–64), and Grade D (Critical Outbreak / <45).

3. Field Extension Ground-Truthing PWA:
  • Mobile-first Progressive Web App with offline SQLite caching for ADOs and Krishi Sakhis.
  • Sub-meter RTK GPS plot coordinate geofencing, pheromone trap moth counting, sweep-net density logging, and on-device Computer Vision leaf disease diagnosis (MobileNet edge classifier).

4. Farmer "Sasya Seva (শস্য সেৱা)" Advisory Portal & Dispatcher:
  • Public vernacular portal delivering hyper-local advisories in Assamese (অসমীয়া) and English.
  • Categorized intervention guidance: Cultural drainage management, biological controls (Trichoderma viride, Pseudomonas fluorescens, Neem NSKE 5%, Scirpo-lure pheromone traps), and calibrated chemical dosages per Assam Bigha ($1\text{ Bigha} = 1337.8\text{ m}^2$) only when economic threshold levels (ETL) are breached.
  • Automated push dispatch via SMS Gateway, WhatsApp Cloud API, and Krishi Call Centers.

5. Agro-Command & Admin Console:
  • Role-based calibration interfaces for ASSAC Remote Sensing Analysts (spectral weighting, drone ingestion), District Agricultural Officers (outbreak bulletin authorization), and AAU scientists (incubation constants).

Field 4: Technical architecture & data security
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(Word Count: ~440 words / Max: 500 words)

[ Multi-Tier Cloud & Edge Architecture ]
SasyaSwasthya is built on an enterprise, cloud-native microservices architecture designed for sovereign deployment within the Assam State Data Centre (ASDC):
  • Data Ingestion Layer: Automated Python Airflow DAG pipelines pulling Copernicus Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface reflectance data, USGS Landsat 8/9, ASSAC drone GeoTIFF orthomosaics, and IMD Agromet REST endpoints.
  • Geospatial & Vector Storage: PostgreSQL 16 with PostGIS 3.4, GeoServer WMS/WFS spatial services, and RasterFrames for distributed satellite imagery tiling.
  • Machine Learning Core:
  • Crop Phenology & Vegetation Masking: Random Forest and U-Net models segmenting crop types across Assam cadastre polygons.
  • Epidemiological Prediction: Random Forest Regressor & LSTM neural network trained on 10-year historical outbreak records from AAU Jorhat and IMD weather logs.
  • Edge Diagnostic Classifier: Quantized MobileNet-V3 model running locally within the PWA for offline leaf lesion recognition.
  • Web Frontend & PWA: React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Vite, and Leaflet 1.9.4 WebGIS engine with hardware-accelerated Canvas/WebGL vector layers.
  • Advisory Delivery Engine: Kafka event queues routing messages to the NIC SMS Gateway, WhatsApp Business Cloud API, and automated voice IVRS.

[ Data Security & Sovereignty ]
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Integrated with State Government Single Sign-On (e-Pramaan), enforcing strict privilege separation between ASSAC analysts, DAOs, ADOs, and public farmers.
  • Cryptographic Audit Logging: Every advisory broadcast, disease risk threshold update, and ground inspection submission is timestamped and signed with SHA-256 hashes, ensuring an immutable evidentiary chain.
  • Data Residency Compliance: All satellite rasters, farmer mobile rosters, and cadastral vectors reside strictly within Indian sovereign data centres adhering to MeitY guidelines.

Field 5: Implementation milestones, budget allocation & Swiss Challenge pathway
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ 12-Month Rollout Roadmap ]
  • Phase 1 (Months 1–3) — Ingestion & Engine Integration: Connect ASSAC Sentinel-2 pipeline, IMD AWS feeds, and AAU disease parameter models. Finalize Assamese linguistic dictionary.
  • Phase 2 (Months 4–6) — 3-District Pilot Deployment: Live field pilots across Kamrup Rural, Nagaon, and Jorhat covering 50,000 Bighas. Deploy Mobile PWA to 120 ADOs and Krishi Sakhis.
  • Phase 3 (Months 7–9) — Model Calibration & Advisory Refinement: Calibrate pest thresholds against Kharif/Sali harvest data. Conduct farmer field days and integrate automated WhatsApp broadcast.
  • Phase 4 (Months 10–12) — State-Wide Enterprise Rollout: Expand to all 35 districts across Assam. Transition production hosting to Assam State Data Centre (ASDC) under the Swiss Challenge procurement framework.
