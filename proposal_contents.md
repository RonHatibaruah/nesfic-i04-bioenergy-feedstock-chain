## Field 1: Problem understanding & user workflow

### Problem Context & Scale
Northeast India generates over 12 million metric tonnes of lignocellulosic biomass annually, predominantly bamboo processing offcuts (Bambusa tulda and Bhaluka), post-kharif Sali paddy straw, mustard stover, and sugarcane bagasse. As home to India's pioneer 2G Bioethanol commercial refinery (Assam Bio-Refinery Pvt. Ltd. - ABRPL, Numaligarh / NRL) and an expanding Compressed Biogas (CBG) network under the SATAT mission, Assam represents a critical bio-economy frontier. However, regional commercial bio-refineries face existential operational vulnerabilities arising from Northeast India's extreme monsoonal climate.

### Current Workflow Bottlenecks & Structural Gaps
1. Severe Monsoon Moisture Infiltration: Assam experiences torrential monsoons from May to October, with 2,000 to 4,000 mm rainfall and sustained relative humidity above 85% to 95%. Feedstock harvested in dry windows must be stored for up to 9 months to feed continuous round-the-clock plant operations. Under traditional open-air stack or unsealed corrugated tin sheds, ambient moisture rapidly infiltrates biomass stacks, driving moisture above 30% to 50%.
2. Biological Decay & Catastrophic Self-Heating Fires: Wet stored biomass rapidly undergoes fungal rotting, depolymerization of cellulose, and catastrophic dry-matter loss of 30% to 45%. Furthermore, intense microbial respiration in damp, densely packed bales triggers violent exothermic self-heating (65°C to 80°C), causing spontaneous combustion fires that destroy entire rural stockpiles.
3. Seasonal Feedstock Starvation & Plant Shutdowns: Wet unpaved village roads, localized floods, rotting inventory, and fragmented manual collection cause regional biomass delivery to collapse during the 5-month monsoon window. Consequently, bio-refinery Plant Load Factor (PLF) plunges below 45% to 55%, inflicting crippling capital amortization losses.
4. Unvalorised Secondary Waste Liabilities: Conversion operations generate severe secondary residues: solid enzymatic hydrolysis lignin cake (exceeding 35,000 tonnes annually per 2G facility) and anaerobic digestion spent digestate (Fermented Organic Manure - FOM), alongside high-COD process effluents. Burning or dumping these streams causes serious environmental non-compliance, overloads effluent treatment plants, and squanders high-value chemical and organic assets.

### Target Users & Beneficiaries
- Bio-Refinery & CBG Plant Operators (e.g., ABRPL Numaligarh, SATAT Developers): Securing guaranteed 365-day feedstock supply at controlled moisture (<15% dry basis or standardized ensilage) and sustaining continuous 90%+ plant load factor.
- Rural Biomass Aggregators & FPOs (50,000+ Farmers): Monetizing crop residues with fair, transparent, moisture-calibrated digital off-take pricing and Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) payments.
- Depot QA Engineers: Standardizing moisture, bale density, and biological stabilization metrics using IoT telemetry.
- Regional Tea Estates & Highway Authorities: Off-taking organic bio-fertilizers and high-performance lignin-modified bio-bitumen asphalt.

### Intended Outcome
JaivikSuraksha bridges raw agricultural realities and continuous industrial biorefining by deploying engineered biological ensiling (anaerobic lactic acid preservation), hermetic IoT-monitored storage pods, parasitic waste-heat drying, and a circular secondary waste valorisation pipeline. Measurable targets include:
- Zero monsoon supply-chain shutdown (maintaining 90%+ bio-refinery utilization).
- Reduction in biomass storage dry-matter loss from >35% to under 4.5%.
- 100% valorisation of lignin cake into IRC:SP:53 bio-bitumen and digestate into certified organic fertilizer.

### Proposed Solution Overview
JaivikSuraksha (জৈৱিক সুৰক্ষা) is an industrial-grade, closed-loop bioenergy supply-chain platform engineering complete weather-resilience for the North-Eastern bio-economy. It integrates biological wet preservation, IoT-instrumented decentralized storage pods, parasitic refinery waste-heat recovery, and secondary waste stream upcycling into a unified operational grid.

### Main Modules & Key Features
1. Climate-Resilient Monsoon Biomass Preservation Suite:
   - Engineered Anaerobic Ensiling for High-Moisture Feedstock: For wet-harvested paddy straw and green bamboo offcuts (moisture 45% to 65%), the system utilizes controlled lactic acid bacteria (Lactobacillus plantarum and Pediococcus acidilactici) ensiling inside oxygen-barrier hermetic bunker silos. Rapid anaerobic acidification drops internal pH to 3.8 to 4.2 within 72 hours, permanently suppressing fungal mold (Aspergillus, Fusarium), halting dry-matter loss, and preserving structural glucan and xylan polymers for 12+ months.
   - Parasitic Waste-Heat Drying & Hydrophobic Densification: Harnesses low-grade waste flue gas (120°C to 160°C) and flash steam from the bio-refinery to drive rotary pre-dryers, bringing seasonal straw to <12% moisture. Dried feedstock is pelletized with a thin bio-derived hydrophobic botanical coating to prevent monsoonal humidity regain.
   - Decentralized Pods with Hermetic IoT Telemetry: A distributed network of raised-plinth cluster depots equipped with multi-depth wireless IoT sensor spears tracking moisture, thermal gradients, and CO2/CH4 headspace levels, triggering automated ventilation before self-heating thresholds are reached.
2. High-Value Biorefinery Secondary Waste Valorisation Engine:
   - Lignin-to-Bio-Bitumen & Green Polymer Upcycling: Thermochemically purifies and micronizes sulfur-free enzymatic hydrolysis lignin cake into a green polymer-modified asphalt extender (replacing 20% of fossil bitumen in Northeast road construction) meeting IRC:SP:53 and IS 73 standards.
   - Enriched Fermented Organic Manure (FOM) Formulator: Converts anaerobic digestate slurry into certified, pathogen-free solid and liquid bio-fertilizers enriched with regional nitrogen-fixing bio-inoculants (Azotobacter and Trichoderma) for tea gardens and organic farming.
   - Parasitic Biomethane Closed Loop: Anaerobic biomethanation of high-COD process effluents to recover supplemental biomethane, routed internally to power biomass drying units and reduce refinery parasitic grid power.
3. Decentralized Supply Chain & Agro-Hub WebGIS:
   - Spatio-temporal GIS platform mapping biomass availability across Assam's 35 districts, optimizing village aggregation radii (15 to 35 km economic haulage limits), scheduling all-weather road logistics, and tracking real-time depot inventory runways (target >60 days buffer).
4. Bilingual Farmer Aggregator Portal ("Jaivik Seva"):
   - Assamese and English progressive web portal connecting smallholders, self-help groups, and FPOs. Features transparent, moisture-calibrated off-take settlements, automated weighbridge integration, and direct bank account payouts via Aadhaar DBT.
5. Industrial SCADA & Compliance Command Console:
   - Centralized control room for plant feedstock officers and environmental regulators featuring tamper-evident SHA-256 cryptographic audit logs tracking feedstock chain-of-custody, life-cycle greenhouse gas (GHG) carbon offsets, and zero-liquid-discharge (ZLD) parameters.

### What Changes Versus Current Approach
Replaces uncoordinated open-air rotting and seasonal refinery shutdowns with a scientifically stabilized, all-weather feedstock reserve and converts expensive waste disposal liabilities into lucrative secondary revenue streams.

### Proposed User Journey Across Key Personas
1. Smallholder Farmer / FPO Harvest Off-Take & Sale:
   A farmer in Bokakhat (Golaghat) harvests 15 Bighas of Sali paddy. Through the Jaivik Seva mobile interface (Assamese/English), the farmer registers 18 MT straw. The GIS algorithm assigns the nearest hub (Hub-Bokakhat East, 8.4 km away) and schedules collection.
2. Depot In-Situ QA & Rapid Inoculation:
   At the depot, the QA Officer measures incoming moisture via calibrated RF probe (52% due to rain). The system routes bales to the Anaerobic Ensiling Silo Pod. Bales are shredded, inoculated with Lactobacillus, compacted into airtight polymer silos, and sealed. The farmer receives an instant weighbridge receipt and digital payment via Aadhaar DBT.
3. Continuous IoT Silo Telemetry & Combustion Guard:
   Over the 4-month monsoon, multi-depth SS316 IoT sensor spears stream real-time data. When Sensor Pod-04 detects temperature rising from 32°C to 50°C and elevated CO2 (early microbial heating), the system triggers an emergency dashboard alert, activating automated N2 purging and vents to avert fire.
4. Biorefinery Feedstock Dispatch & Continuous Plant Load:
   Central Supply Command at Numaligarh monitors depot buffers. During July floods when remote transit is cut, the refinery draws steadily from ensiled reserves and hydrophobic briquettes at the 15-day buffer hub, sustaining 92% plant utilization.
5. Waste Stream Upcycling & Circular Revenue:
   As enzymatic hydrolysis yields 120 daily tonnes of lignin cake and 450 m³ spent wash, the facility diverts lignin to produce bio-bitumen batches (BATCH-LIGNIN-2026-04). Digestate slurry is separated into certified organic manure (FOM) for regional tea estates and logged into a SHA-256 audit ledger.

### Full-Stack Software Stack & Cloud Infrastructure
- Frontend Presentation Layer: React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Vite, and Lucide Icons. Designed for ultra-responsive desktop command consoles and offline-capable mobile field devices adhering to GIGW accessibility standards.
- Geospatial & Supply Chain Mapping: Leaflet 1.9.4 GIS engine, GeoJSON vector pipelines, Carto Light / OpenStreetMap basemaps, and OGC-compliant spatial queries mapping Assam biomass clusters within 35 km economic transport buffers.
- Backend Infrastructure & APIs: Python 3.11 FastAPI asynchronous framework, GeoPandas spatial vector analysis, NetworkX logistics routing, and Celery task workers for distributed IoT telemetry processing.

- Edge Deployment & Hosting: Cloudflare Pages and Edge Workers delivering sub-50ms latency across Northeast India with automated SSL, HTTP/2, edge caching, and DDoS defense.

### IoT Sensor Telemetry & Hardware Specifications
- IoT Smart Probe Spears: 2.2m stainless steel (SS316) probe shafts containing multi-level digital temperature sensors (DS18B20, ±0.1°C accuracy) and capacitive biomass moisture sensors (capacitive frequency domain reflectometry - FDR, 5% to 80% range).
- Headspace Gas Safety Sensors: Electrochemical CO, non-dispersive infrared (NDIR) CO2 (0 to 50,000 ppm), and semiconductor CH4 gas sensors mounted inside silo plenums to detect microbial fermentation spikes and exothermic fire precursors.
- Wireless Edge Gateways: Solar-powered low-power wireless nodes utilizing Semtech SX1262 LoRaWAN (865 to 867 MHz India band) transmitting telemetry up to 12 km to depot base stations, equipped with dual 4G-LTE/NavIC fallback.
- Automated Weather Station (AWS) Integration: Real-time ingestion of IMD and state meteorological feeds tracking ambient humidity, rainfall rate, and dew-point oscillations to dynamically adjust depot ventilation schedules.

### Biochemical & Chemical Process Engineering
- Microbial Inoculant Formulation: Lyophilized bacterial consortium comprising Lactobacillus plantarum (ATCC 8014) and Pediococcus acidilactici applied at 10^6 CFU/g fresh biomass with 1.5% molasses carrier to initiate rapid homofermentative lactic conversion.
- Parasitic Thermal Drying Loop: Direct-indirect rotary triple-pass drum dryer utilizing refinery stack flue gas (140°C) to reduce paddy straw moisture from 28% to <12% at an energy consumption of <2.8 MJ/kg water evaporated.
- Lignin Depolymerization & Compounding: High-shear mechanical homogenization of enzymatic hydrolysis lignin (Klason lignin content >82%, ash <2.5%) with heavy vacuum residue asphalt (VG-30) at 160°C to generate high-performance bio-bitumen meeting IS 73:2018 and IRC:SP:53 specifications.
- Biomass Quality & Rot Vulnerability Index (BQVI): Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) composite scoring model evaluating moisture, fungal titer, thermal gradient, and glucan retention.

### Data Flow & Cryptographic Interoperability
Data flows cyclically: Farmer harvest booking -> Depot QA moisture/ensilage verification -> IoT sensor telemetry monitoring -> Automated aeration/purging control -> Biorefinery supply dispatch -> Waste stream mass-balance tracking -> Cryptographic SHA-256 audit ledger logging for carbon credits and environmental compliance.

### Methodology Overview
The execution of JaivikSuraksha follows an Agile, Stage-Gate Systems Engineering framework tailored to heavy industrial bioenergy supply chains and public infrastructure. The project is structured across 6 phased stages over a 9-month execution roadmap:

### Six-Stage Execution Lifecycle & Intermediate Outputs
1. Discovery, Feedstock Characterization & Agro-Climatic Mapping (Months 1-2):
   - Activities: Field surveys across 5 priority bioenergy districts (Golaghat/Numaligarh, Nagaon, Kamrup Rural, Barpeta, Tinsukia). Laboratory biochemical characterization of dominant regional feedstocks (paddy straw, Bambusa tulda residues, mustard stover, tea waste) measuring structural carbohydrates (glucan, xylan), lignin, moisture absorption isotherms, and seasonal harvest curves. Stakeholder workshops with MC² Foundation, Assam Bio-Refinery Pvt. Ltd. (ABRPL), and local farmer producer organizations (FPOs).
   - Key Outputs: Formal System Requirements Specification (SRS); regional feedstock supply-shed GIS atlas; baseline biomass degradation kinetic models under monsoonal humidity.
2. Biological Ensiling & Parasitic Drying Bench Optimization (Months 2-3):
   - Activities: Bench and pilot-scale trials of anaerobic lactic ensiling using micro-inoculants on high-moisture straw (50% to 60% moisture). Testing carbohydrate retention, lactic/acetic acid ratios, and fungal mold inhibition across 90-day storage simulations. Prototype design of parasitic flue-gas flash-drying integration.
   - Key Outputs: Standardized ensiling operational manual; validated dry-matter loss <5%; drying energy-balance model.
3. IoT Sensor Spear Engineering & Cloud WebGIS Development (Months 3-4):
   - Activities: Fabrication and calibration of LoRaWAN-enabled SS316 IoT sensor spears for moisture, temperature, and CO2 monitoring. Development of the full-stack React/TypeScript WebGIS dashboard, decentralized depot management interface, and bilingual farmer PWA.
   - Key Outputs: 10 fully tested IoT sensor probe units; operational WebGIS platform on Cloudflare Pages; integration with IMD weather APIs.
4. Waste Stream Valorisation Pilot Formulations (Months 4-6):
   - Activities: Sourcing raw enzymatic hydrolysis lignin cake from 2G bio-refinery pilot facilities. Formulating bio-bitumen binders (15%, 20%, 25% lignin substitution in VG-30 bitumen) and conducting Marshall stability, rutting resistance, and ductility testing per IRC/IS standards. Formulating enriched bio-fertilizers from anaerobic digestate (FOM).
   - Key Outputs: Certified bio-bitumen performance test report (Marshall stability 14.8 kN); enriched organic bio-fertilizer formulation for tea garden trials.
5. Integrated Depot Field Pilot & Biorefinery Stress Testing (Months 6-8):
   - Activities: Commissioning a 500-metric-tonne decentralized pilot biomass depot in Golaghat near Numaligarh. Operating two 250-tonne hermetic ensiling bunker pods and dry IoT-monitored briquette stacks during peak active monsoon rains (June-August). Real-time off-take coordination with refinery boilers.
   - Key Outputs: Zero-loss monsoon preservation demonstration (dry-matter loss verified <4.5%); continuous off-take supply verified under torrential rainfall conditions; complete environmental safety compliance audit.
6. Commercial Scale-Up, Policy Handover & Industrial Rollout (Month 9):
   - Activities: Finalizing commercial supply agreements with bio-refinery off-takers and regional FPOs. Handover of the operational SCADA control room and citizen platform to project partners; publication of open-access deployment blueprints.
   - Key Outputs: Production-ready platform deployment; signed off-take framework agreements; complete technical and commercial sustainability dossier.

### Required Data Streams & Information Inputs
JaivikSuraksha relies strictly on verified public datasets, open-access meteorological APIs, standardized laboratory analytical protocols, and authenticated edge sensor streams:

1. Geospatial & Land Administration Data:
   - Cadastral & Revenue Boundaries: Village-level boundary shapefiles and revenue circle vector layers from Assam State Space Applications Centre (ASSAC) and Dharitree portal (WFS/GeoJSON format) to establish 15 to 35 km economic haulage buffer polygons around cluster depots.
   - Crop Acreage & Biomass Density: District agricultural statistics from Directorate of Economics & Statistics, Assam, covering Sali paddy, Boro paddy, mustard, and sugarcane acreage (Tabular CSV/JSON).
   - Road Infrastructure Network: OpenStreetMap (OSM) and Assam PWD highway shapefiles for routing heavy trucks around flood-vulnerable lowlands.
2. Meteorological & Agro-Climatic Data:
   - High-Resolution Weather Telemetry: India Meteorological Department (IMD) Open AWS API streaming hourly rainfall (mm), ambient temperature (°C), relative humidity (%), wind velocity, and solar radiation (JSON REST API).
   - Flood Inundation Warnings: Central Water Commission (CWC) Brahmaputra river gauge data and Assam State Disaster Management Authority (ASDMA) flood alert bulletins (REST/GeoJSON).
3. Field IoT Sensor & Telemetry Data:
   - Multi-Depth Probe Readings: 15-minute sensor payloads transmitted via LoRaWAN (865-867 MHz) containing 4-point internal temperature (DS18B20), 4-point moisture content (capacitive FDR, % wet basis), and plenum headspace gas concentrations (NDIR CO2 ppm, electrochemical CO ppm, CH4 ppm).
   - Automated Weighbridge Telemetry: Digital RS-232/Modbus serial payloads from depot weighbridge scales capturing gross weight, tare weight, and vehicle registration numbers.
4. Laboratory Analytical & Chemical Characterization Data:
   - Feedstock Composition: National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL) LAP analytical assays measuring glucan, xylan, galactan, arabinan, Klason lignin, and acid-soluble ash contents.
   - Bitumen & Organic Manure Assays: Mechanical test curves (penetration, softening point, Marshall stability kN) per IS 1202/1205/ASTM D6927; heavy metal and NPK spectra per Fertilizer Control Order (FCO 1985).

### Processing, Annotation & Data Quality Control
- Telemetry Ingestion Pipeline: Incoming LoRaWAN hex packets decoded by ChirpStack, validated against timestamped checksums, normalized, and ingested into TimescaleDB with automated outlier rejection (e.g., negative moisture spikes).
- Privacy-Preserving Tokenization: Farmer identifying data (Aadhaar, bank account numbers) is never stored directly in spatial databases. Telemetry links only to pseudonymous Farmer IDs, with payouts processed via encrypted tokenized hashes.

### Dependencies & Realistic Access Assumptions
The architecture relies entirely on open APIs (IMD, OSM, Sentinel open access) and local sensor telemetry. No unconfirmed proprietary access to restricted government databases is required. Bio-refinery residue parameters utilize standardized NREL/published 2G bioethanol benchmarks with non-disclosure agreement (NDA) validation from ABRPL.
